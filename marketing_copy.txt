HOOK: "Where are we with Client X?" answered from their last call transcript, their open tickets and their deploy log, not from whoever happened to be in the meeting.

SUBHEADLINE: For tech leads at 20-to-100-person software shops who were told to build the company's AI brain this quarter and have no budget line to buy one.

VALUE_PROP:
One status answer per enterprise client, assembled from meeting transcripts, tickets, logs and that client's own deployment config.
Runs on your servers with the model endpoint you choose. No vendor call, no procurement, no per-seat license.
Connectors live as code in your repo, so you extend them with Claude Code instead of waiting on a settings page.

CTA: Run it on one client

SECONDARY_CTA: Read the architecture first

OBJECTIONS:
- "Onyx is free and Hyper already exists. Why this?" Both index Slack and docs and answer "find me the document"; neither keys every source to a specific client and its deployment, which is the question your CTO actually asks on Monday.
- "I can build this myself with Claude Code in a weekend." The chat box is the weekend; keeping transcripts, tickets and logs current, deduplicated and permissioned per client is the quarter, and that is the part shipped here.
- "Our enterprise clients will not let their data touch a US SaaS." Nothing leaves your VPC except calls to the model endpoint you configure, and you can point that at a local model if the contract requires it.
